# Orange Taxi 

Build a premium, modern, highly polished website for a new Indian electric mobility company called Orange Taxi.

BRAND CONCEPT

Orange Taxi provides a premium taxi experience at a more accessible price than traditional luxury/premium cab services.

The core positioning is:

“Premium, without the premium price.”

Orange Taxi should feel significantly more premium than a normal taxi service, while still feeling friendly, approachable, warm and affordable.

The customer should immediately feel:

“This looks premium and luxurious, but it doesn't feel overpriced or intimidating.”

Do NOT make it look like Uber, Ola, or a generic taxi-booking template.

The brand should feel like a modern premium mobility/lifestyle company.

BRAND PERSONALITY

The visual and UX personality should be:

Premium

Elegant

Friendly

Warm

Trustworthy

Modern

Human

Effortless

Accessible

Sustainable

Avoid making the brand feel:

Cheap

Corporate

Overly futuristic

Aggressively tech-focused

Generic

Cluttered

Like a copy of Uber/Ola

COLOR DIRECTION

Use Orange Taxi's orange as the primary brand accent, but DO NOT make the entire website orange.

Preferred visual balance:

Warm white / ivory backgrounds

Deep charcoal / near-black typography

Orange as the primary accent

Very subtle neutral grey tones

Optional muted green accents for EV/sustainability information

Orange should primarily appear in:

Primary CTA buttons

Highlights

Small visual accents

Icons

Important UI states

Brand details

The overall website should feel premium through whitespace, typography, photography and composition rather than excessive color.

WEBSITE STRUCTURE

Create a complete responsive homepage with the following sections:

1. NAVIGATION

Minimal, elegant navigation.

Logo:
ORANGE TAXI

Navigation:

Home

Experience

Electric

For Business

Drive With Us

About

Right side:

Book a Ride — primary orange CTA

The navbar should become sticky on scroll with a subtle background/blur effect.

2. HERO SECTION

Create a visually stunning hero section.

Main headline:

Premium rides.
Without the premium price.

Supporting text:

“Premium electric cars, professional drivers and thoughtful service — designed to make every journey feel a little better.”

Primary CTA:

Book a Ride

Secondary CTA:

Explore the Orange Experience

Hero visual:

Show a beautiful premium orange electric vehicle in a sophisticated urban environment.

The vehicle should feel premium, clean and aspirational.

Use large cinematic imagery and generous whitespace.

Add subtle animation/parallax effects, but keep them elegant and performance-conscious.

3. VALUE PROPOSITION

Section headline:

More than a ride.

Subheading:

“It's the little things that make a journey feel different.”

Create 4 premium feature cards:

Complimentary Water

“A little refreshment, waiting when you arrive.”

Pre-Cooled Cabin

“Step into a comfortable car, not a hot one.”

Professional Drivers

“Trained, courteous and committed to better service.”

Premium Electric Vehicles

“Quiet, smooth and thoughtfully maintained.”

Use beautiful icons and/or photography.

Avoid generic icon-heavy SaaS design.

4. ORANGE EXPERIENCE

Create a large editorial-style section showcasing the passenger experience.

Headline:

The premium experience, made everyday.

Show a sequence of moments:

Your car arrives clean and ready.

Your driver greets you professionally.

A bottle of water is waiting.

The cabin is cool and comfortable.

Your journey is quiet and smooth.

You arrive feeling taken care of.

Use large images, subtle scroll animations and strong typography.

Make this section feel like a premium hospitality brand rather than a taxi company.

5. PRICE / VALUE SECTION

This is extremely important.

Headline:

Premium shouldn't mean overpriced.

Explain that Orange Taxi aims to deliver a premium experience at a significantly more accessible price than traditional premium chauffeur/taxi services.

Create a visual comparison without attacking competitors.

Example:

Everyday Cab

Basic transportation

Orange Taxi

Premium EV + thoughtful service + professional driver

Luxury Chauffeur

Premium experience + significantly higher price

Position Orange Taxi visually in the middle:

Premium experience
Accessible price

Do not invent exact prices or competitor prices.

Use placeholder pricing where necessary.

6. ELECTRIC VEHICLE SECTION

Headline:

Quiet rides. Cleaner cities.

Supporting text:

“Every Orange ride is electric — giving you a quieter, smoother journey while helping build cleaner cities.”

Show a premium orange EV.

Create small metrics/cards such as:

100% Electric Fleet

Quiet & Smooth

Lower Running Costs

Designed for the Future

Do not make the site feel like an environmental NGO. EV should support the premium experience, not dominate the brand.

7. PREMIUM VEHICLE SECTION

Create a visually rich vehicle showcase.

Headline:

Display the available vehicle class as a premium card.

Example:

Complimentary water

Professional driver

Smooth electric ride

CTA:

Book Orange Premium

Do not invent specific vehicle brands/models unless explicitly provided later.

8. DRIVER SECTION

Headline:

Great rides start with great people.

Explain that Orange Taxi works with professionally trained drivers and focuses on consistent service quality.

Include:

Professional training

Customer-first service

Safety-focused driving

Service standards

Fair employment/performance model

CTA:

Drive With Orange

9. CORPORATE SECTION

Headline:

Better rides for your business.

Supporting text:

“Give your employees, clients and guests a more comfortable way to travel.”

Features:

Corporate bookings

Employee transportation

Airport transfers

CTA:

Book Your Orange

Drive With Orange

UX REQUIREMENTS

The website must feel exceptionally polished and easy to use.

Prioritize:

Clear visual hierarchy

Large readable typography

Generous whitespace

Strong photography

Simple navigation

Clear CTAs

Mobile-first responsive design

Fast loading

Accessibility

Smooth but subtle animations

Excellent hover states

Excellent mobile interactions

Do not over-animate.

The design should feel premium even when animations are disabled.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Design carefully for:

Desktop

Laptop

Tablet

Mobile

The mobile version must not simply be a compressed desktop layout.

Create a dedicated mobile navigation experience.

CTAs should be easy to tap.

Typography and spacing should remain elegant on small screens.

UI DESIGN LANGUAGE

Use:

Large editorial typography

Rounded but sophisticated cards

High-quality imagery

Subtle shadows

Soft borders

Premium spacing

Elegant transitions

Minimal icons

High-quality micro-interactions

Avoid:

Excessive gradients

Excessive glassmorphism

Generic SaaS cards

Too many icons

Neon colors

Excessive orange

Cheap-looking illustrations

Template-like sections

The website should look like a premium mobility brand, not an AI-generated website.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

Build this as a production-quality responsive React web application.

Use a clean component architecture.

Create reusable components for:

Navbar

Buttons

Feature cards

Vehicle cards

Testimonial cards

FAQ accordion

CTA sections

Footer

Responsive navigation

Keep the code modular and maintainable because this website will later be connected to:

Passenger booking system

Passenger mobile app

Driver app

Driver onboarding

Admin dashboard

Backend APIs

Booking and payment systems

Do not build fake backend functionality yet.

For booking CTAs, create clean placeholder routes/components that can later connect to the real booking system.

IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The most important objective is:

Make Orange Taxi look MORE premium than its price suggests.

The website should make a visitor think:

“This feels like a luxury service.”

Then immediately communicate:

“But it's actually affordable enough to use regularly.”

The emotional hierarchy should be:

Premium → Trust → Comfort → Value → EV

NOT:

Cheap → EV → Taxi

Orange Taxi is selling an experience, not simply transportation.

Create the final website with the polish and attention to detail expected from a top-tier modern mobility startup.
